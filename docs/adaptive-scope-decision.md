# Adaptive-demo scope decision against the original TASKS.md stop gate (for Kumar, 2026-10-10)

TASKS.md gate (Phase 1 "Vocabulary coverage"): ≥ 60% of scoring criteria typed; 40-60% expand vocabulary; **< 40% STOP and reassess the adaptive demo scope with Kumar.** Measured strict typed coverage: 5.7% at the gate, 3.3% (16/488) on the 34 prepared-profile trials (`docs/coverage-audit.md`). This note is that reassessment. The gate is **not met and stays not met under either option**; neither option marks it passed. Estimates below are estimates, not measurements.

## Option A: meet the 40% threshold
What it takes (strict metric unchanged, one criterion = typed only if every leaf is an executable atom):
- Ceiling check from the audit: even if every short single-proposition criterion about an existing key were typed, that is about **9%** (45/488). L1+L2 add about 4 points. Reaching 40% (about 195 of 488) needs three things together: (1) typing **compound** criteria (and/or/except blocks) through the existing clause blocks while keeping the soundness checks, which today refuse them by design; (2) a **much larger vocabulary** (the 36 keys cover 46% of criteria by cue word only, and most of those are compound; infection, comorbidity, other labs, concomitant medication, surgery history and blood pressure have no keys: 133 of the 472 untyped criteria contain no cue word at all); (3) parser prompt changes and **paid re-parses** with a false-typed review at each step.
- Even at 40%, **lifts are not guaranteed**: UNCERTAIN→POSSIBLE needs every core criterion of a trial fully parsed (8 of 34 trials are within 1-2 repairs today). The gate does not measure that, so a second measure (actual lifts after a full re-evaluation) would still decide whether the adaptive demo works.
- Realistic schedule (my estimate): **3-4 weeks** of vocabulary and logic work in at least 3 measure-and-fix cycles, each with a paid re-parse of a development cohort and one single-use untouched-cohort validation at the end; success is not assured, and 40% may not be reachable because many criteria are not facts a visitor can state. This lands after the Phase 5 start (Oct 22) and displaces the eval harness and abstention set (never-cut items).
- Lines changed: **TASKS Phase 1 "Vocabulary coverage"** (stays open, gate must actually reach ≥ 40% before Phase 3 proceeds); **TASKS Phase 3** "Counterfactual question engine" (needs the missing monotonicity test) and "Answer-handling path" (approved scope: full re-evaluation, about 41 calls, 60-90 s); **SPEC §8 #4** "tiers visibly update instantly" (changes anyway: a full re-evaluation is not instant; becomes "after a re-evaluation, tiers can update"); **SPEC §5 step 7** (typed re-tier plus targeted re-run → full re-evaluation); Phase 5 start date and the eval/abstention items slip.

## Option B: "What to ask the study team" panel (truthful, no tier-change promise)
Experience: after results, an optional panel lists up to 3 open items that block the most trials, each with the trials it affects and why the item matters, then says only a study team can answer it. It reads, for example: "Most of the unclear results depend on these open items. A study team can confirm them." There is **no answer step, no re-tiering, no count or promise of movement**. Items come from the open scoring criteria and `depends_on` keys of free-text leaves, not only typed atoms (wider reach; at current coverage it always has content: in the 34-trial cohort `ecog` 7, `her2_status` 4, `measurable_disease` 3, `prior_other_malignancy` 3, `er_status` 2, `pr_status` 2 text criteria depend on them). Pregnancy-related items stay excluded. Pathology-confirmation and similar documentation requirements appear as "ask the study team", never as passed. Copy follows `docs/copy-rules.md` (R2, no verified language).
- Schedule: about **1-1.5 days** (ranking by affected trials, panel, copy audit, tests), removes the answer path and the paid full re-evaluation from Phase 3; Phase 5 start unchanged.
- Lines changed: **SPEC §8 #4** becomes "Adaptive panel names the most decisive open items for the study team; no answer step; no tier change"; **SPEC §5** steps 3-5 (counterfactual gain ranking) and 7 (answer update) are dropped for this panel and the "monotonicity" unit test no longer applies; the zero-gain filter is kept for any future "may clarify" variant; **TASKS Phase 3** "Counterfactual question engine" (ranking by affected trials) and "Answer-handling path" (removed, not deferred); **TASKS Phase 4** "Screen 4 Results (... adaptive panel)" (panel = study-team questions); **TASKS "Never cut: adaptive questioning"** is reworded to "study-team question panel": an explicit weakening that needs Kumar's written approval. **TASKS Phase 1 "Vocabulary coverage"** stays **not met** and is recorded as a deviation, not a pass; SPEC §8 #5 (coordinator question list) is unchanged.

## Recommendation
**B for the demo.** It is the only option that is true at current coverage and fits the schedule; A cannot be shown to pass inside the window and would not by itself deliver a visible lift. Keep A as a post-demo research track with the untouched cohort preserved. Parser expansion and paid re-parsing stay paused until you choose; choosing A later would restart from the frozen design below.

## Untouched cohort
None is selected. The three prepared profiles' 34 trials and the earlier development cohorts are **development data** and may be used for design. A validation cohort is selected only AFTER a design is frozen, by a rule written down first (fixed ClinicalTrials.gov query, fixed order, N, exclusion of every NCT id already seen), with its list hash recorded in TASKS.md before any parse; no one inspects its criteria text before the freeze; it is parsed once with the frozen design and never used for tuning. No trial outside the development set has been read in this work.
