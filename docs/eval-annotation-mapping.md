## 2. Names: what each number may be called
- **Agreement under TrialGPT's conventions**: direct comparison of a TrialLens finding with `expert_eligibility` after the mapping in 3. It includes TrialGPT's convention of assuming absent facts are not true. It is **not clinical accuracy** and is never described as accuracy.
- **Agreement on reviewer-adjudicated rows**: the headline for any reviewed result. Scorable rows = **every reviewed row for which the reviewers reach a clear adjudicated call of PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN, including calls that contradict `expert_eligibility`** (UNCLEAR only when the reviewers cannot adjudicate, section 10). The pooled figure is always shown with **every evidence category shown separately** (derived from the basis and the expert-sentence status, not from the call; section 4) and with the reviewer-versus-expert contradiction rate. **A headline is never computed on a subset selected by evidence category or by agreement with the expert label.** The earlier ES-only accuracy and the name "TrialLens criterion accuracy" are retired.
- A claim stronger than "agreement on reviewer-adjudicated rows" (for example accuracy against a clinical standard) needs a suitably qualified clinical review defined in the review protocol (section 10) and approved by Kumar; without it the wording stays as above and says it is not clinical accuracy.
- Anything from author-created cases is a development result and is never published as a headline.

## 3. Label mapping (convention layer; used for "agreement under TrialGPT's conventions")
TrialLens finding semantics: inclusion criterion, PASS = the patient meets it; exclusion criterion, PASS = the patient is NOT excluded, FAIL = the patient meets the exclusion.

| `expert_eligibility` (verified TrialGPT vocabulary; confirm the dataset uses it **[needs file]**) | Criterion type | Mapped label |
|---|---|---|
| included | inclusion | PASS |
| not included | inclusion | FAIL |
| excluded | exclusion | FAIL |
| not excluded | exclusion | PASS |
| not enough information | either | UNKNOWN |
| not applicable | either | NOT_APPLICABLE (bucket X5, not scored) |

The mapped label says nothing about whether the note actually contains evidence. That is decided in section 4, not here.

## 4. `expert_sentences`, independent calls and evidence categories
- **A nonempty `expert_sentences` is not automatic proof of PASS/FAIL.** The experts may have selected related sentences that do not establish the criterion, or sentences behind "not enough information". **An empty `expert_sentences` is not automatic proof of an unsupported assumption.** (The field is published as a string; the serialization is handled in section 9. An empty list `"[]"` means empty; an unrecognized serialization is reported as unknown and is never guessed.) It is only a *candidate indicator* for stratification and sampling and for the second review step; it never sets gold.
- **The gold is the independent adjudicated call.** For every reviewed row the reviewers' adjudicated call (PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN, type already applied) **is the gold for the headline, whether or not it agrees with `expert_eligibility`.** A call that contradicts the expert label stays in the scorable denominator and is counted in a separate reviewer-versus-expert contradiction statistic (never removed, never reassigned to match the expert).
- **Evidence categories are derived separately from the call, after adjudication, by fixed rules, and are exhaustive: every Scorable row falls in exactly one.** They are used to show results by category, never to choose rows. Inputs: the adjudicated call, the adjudicated **basis** (explicit statement / inference from stated facts / partial information, not enough to decide / information absent from the note / undetermined) and the **expert-sentence status** (the serialization status from the inspector, plus the Step B answer when sentences were shown). First matching rule wins:
  1. **UNDETERMINED_BASIS**: the reviewers could not settle the basis (the call is still adjudicated and scorable).
  2. **ABSENT**: the adjudicated basis is information absent from the note (whatever the sentences say; a PASS/FAIL call with this basis breaks the reviewer rule and is counted as such).
  3. **PARTIAL_INFORMATION**: the adjudicated call is UNKNOWN because the note only partly addresses the criterion.
  4. otherwise the call is PASS or FAIL on an explicit or inferred basis, and the category follows the expert sentences: **SUPPORTED_BY_EXPERT_SENTENCES** (sentences shown and judged to establish the call); **SUPPORTED_BY_NOTE_NO_EXPERT_SENTENCES** (the expert sentences are empty or null, so none were shown: *a clear note-supported call with an empty list stays here and is scored*); **SUPPORTED_BY_NOTE_SENTENCES_UNREADABLE** (the serialization is unknown, so none could be shown); **NOTE_SUPPORTED_EXPERT_SENTENCES_NOT_ESTABLISHING** (sentences shown but related-without-establishing or unrelated).
  Exhaustiveness is checked offline before any row is read (`eval/evidence_categories.py`, `eval/test_evidence_categories.py`: all 3 x 5 x 5 combinations of call, basis and sentence status map to exactly one category and every category is reachable). A category never changes the call.
- **UNCLEAR is used only when the reviewers cannot adjudicate the row** (section 10). An UNCLEAR row is excluded from the scorable denominator and counted by stratum.
- An unreviewed row is never promoted to gold, and a reviewed call is never reassigned afterwards.

## 5. Exclusions with a precedence rule (a row falls in exactly one bucket)
Each row is assigned to the **first** matching bucket in this order; counts therefore partition D0 and cannot overlap:
1. **X1 MALFORMED**: required fields missing or unreadable (label, identifiers, criterion text).
2. **X2 TRAINING-EXCLUDED**: exists only if the authors' documentation defines `training` in a way that makes some rows unsuitable for evaluation (section 6); while the meaning is undefined this bucket is empty and the flag is only a stratifier.
3. **X3 UNALIGNED**: no exact normalized-text match to a TrialLens criterion (`splitTrialCriteria`); merged or split criteria included. No fuzzy matching, no relabelling. The alignment rate is reported.
4. **X4 NON-SCORING**: a TrialLens non-scoring criterion (consent/logistics) or a criterion whose only supporting text is TrialGPT's appended consent sentence.
5. **X5 NOT_APPLICABLE**: `expert_eligibility` = not applicable.
6. **X6 CONTESTED**: exists only if the file has per-annotator labels **[needs file]**; otherwise this bucket does not exist.
Remainder = **D_conv**, the rows used for "agreement under TrialGPT's conventions". Identity checked in code later: `D0 = X1 + X2 + X3 + X4 + X5 + X6 + D_conv`. A diagnostic reports how often TrialLens committed PASS/FAIL on X5 rows (it has no not-applicable status); it is not a score.

## 6. The `training` flag (meaning NOT established)
- **Sources checked for a definition (2026-10-07):** the TrialGPT repository README (full text), the code in `trialgpt_matching`, `trialgpt_ranking` and `trialgpt_retrieval`, `requirements.txt`, and the repository file listing: **none mentions a `training` field or flag.** The Nature Communications paper and the Hugging Face card are not reachable from this environment, and Kumar reports that the published README lists `training` without defining it. So the meaning is **unknown**: it could mark rows used to calibrate annotators, to design prompts, or something else.
- **Therefore `training == False` is NOT called held-out, and no number is described as held-out.** Until the authors define it, the flag is a **stratifier only**: counts and per-stratum results are reported for each value, never pooled silently, and never labelled train or test.
- **No tuning on any of these rows:** TrialLens prompts, rules, thresholds and this mapping are not adjusted using any annotation row, regardless of the flag. Any change motivated by looking at rows is logged here and the rows are then reported as development rows.
- **To establish the meaning** (not a blocker for aggregate inspection): the Methods/Data text of the Nature Communications paper on the 1,015 annotations, the full dataset README text around `training`, or an answer from the authors (GitHub issue on ncbi-nlp/TrialGPT or a Hugging Face discussion). If it turns out to mark rows unsuitable for evaluation, those rows move to bucket X2; if it marks a genuine training/held-out split, the held-out rows become a pre-declared stratum and the rule is logged here before use.

## 7. Denominators and rates
D0 = all rows; D_conv = D0 minus the exclusion buckets; Reviewed = rows given independent reviews; **Scorable = Reviewed rows with a clear adjudicated call (PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN), regardless of agreement with `expert_eligibility`**. Every rate prints numerator and denominator; a zero denominator prints "n/a". Coverage (the share of D_conv that is typed/fully parsed) is printed beside every number, and results are stratified (never pooled across cohorts, criterion types or parse status) as well as shown overall.
- **Agreement under TrialGPT's conventions** = rows where the TrialLens finding equals the mapped `expert_eligibility` label / D_conv (AMBIGUOUS folds into UNKNOWN). Separate figure, separate name.
- **Agreement on reviewer-adjudicated rows (headline)** = Scorable rows where the TrialLens finding equals the adjudicated call / all Scorable rows. Always printed together with the same ratio **for each evidence category separately** (the seven categories of section 4), the count of UNCLEAR rows, and the **reviewer-versus-expert contradiction rate** (Scorable rows where the adjudicated call differs from the mapped expert label / Scorable rows). One is never shown without the others.
- **Unsupported-assumption rate** = committed PASS/FAIL predictions on Scorable rows whose adjudicated call is UNKNOWN / all committed predictions on Scorable rows.
- **False PASS on exclusion criteria** (a criterion-level metric, not a statement about patients or trials) = exclusion criteria whose adjudicated call is FAIL (the criterion applies) predicted PASS / exclusion criteria whose adjudicated call is FAIL; also reported for the convention label.
- **Reviewer agreement** (between the independent reviewers) is reported with the headline. **Per-annotator agreement of the dataset's experts is not calculated** unless the downloaded file really contains per-annotator labels.
- Sampling note: if the reviewed rows are a sample, the headline is for that sample with its stated size and seed (section 10), not for all 1,015 rows.
- No claim of beating TrialGPT or any published system; no result is published without Kumar's review.

## 10. Review protocol, draft v0 (for approval BEFORE any row text is read; items that depend on the aggregate counts are pending)
1. **Reviewers and independence.** At least two reviewers per row, working separately, **blind to each other, to any TrialLens output and to `expert_eligibility`** throughout.
2. **Two steps, to avoid prompting the first judgment. The basis options in Step A are: explicit statement / inference from stated facts / partial information / information absent.**
   - **Step A (before `expert_sentences` is shown):** the reviewer sees the **criterion type (inclusion or exclusion), the criterion text and the note** and records the **initial call** (PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN, type already applied; or CANNOT_JUDGE if the note and criterion do not allow a call) and the **evidence basis** (explicit statement / inference from stated facts / information absent from the note). Rule given to reviewers: the type is shown because it fixes what PASS and FAIL mean (inclusion: PASS = meets; exclusion: PASS = the exclusion does not apply, FAIL = it applies). If the note neither states nor lets you infer the fact, the call is UNKNOWN; do not assume that absence means "not true". The type comes from the dataset's own field if it has one, otherwise from the trial record's section (the label vocabulary alone cannot give it for `not enough information` and `not applicable`); which source applies is settled by the schema inspection.
   - **Step B:** where the expert sentences are nonempty and readable, the tool then shows them and the reviewer records whether the sentences **establish** the call, are **related but do not establish** it, are **unrelated**, or **none were provided**. Rows with empty/null/unreadable sentences skip Step B; the tool records the status automatically (it is never entered by the reviewer). The Step A call is the call of record. A reviewer may change it only by logging a revision with a reason ("overlooked text in the note"); both values are kept and the revision rate is reported.
3. **Adjudicated call (the gold).** If the two reviewers' final calls agree and are PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN, that is the adjudicated call. If they differ, a third reviewer, who sees neither earlier call nor reasoning, decides; a call held by at least two of the three is the adjudicated call. **UNCLEAR only when no call can be adjudicated:** no majority among three, or a majority of reviewers answered CANNOT_JUDGE. Disagreement about the **basis** alone never makes a row UNCLEAR: the third reviewer settles the basis; if still no majority the basis is UNDETERMINED_BASIS and the row stays scorable.
4. **Evidence category (derived by fixed rules, separately from the call, after adjudication):** one of the seven exhaustive categories of section 4, from the adjudicated basis, the expert-sentence status and the Step B answers. A row whose adjudicated call contradicts `expert_eligibility` keeps its call and category and is counted as a contradiction.
5. **Qualifications.** For the headline wording "agreement on reviewer-adjudicated rows": reviewers who can read an eligibility criterion and a clinical note, with the project author acceptable as one reviewer (non-clinical wording only). **For any claim beyond that wording, at least one reviewer per row must be a licensed physician, ideally with oncology or trial-screening experience, whose identity and credentials are recorded; the claim is then worded as agreement with that clinical review, still not a validated clinical accuracy.** Kumar names the reviewers (not decided here).
6. **Reviewer statistics reported with the headline:** reviewer-to-reviewer agreement on the call and on the basis (a reviewer statistic, not the dataset annotators'), revision rate, UNCLEAR count by stratum.
7. **Sampling (concrete proposal; the per-stratum numbers need the aggregate counts, which are not available yet).** Code: `eval/sampling_plan.py` (pure; tested offline in `eval/test_sampling_plan.py`; it has not been run on any real row id).
   - **Strata:** label x criterion type x evidence-list status (empty / nonempty / null / unknown-serialization) x `training` value, exactly the cells of the inspector's cross-tab.
   - **Parameters (proposed):** seed **`20261030`**; total review budget **240 rows** (each reviewed by two independent reviewers, plus a third on disagreements: roughly 240 x 2 x about 4 minutes, about 32 reviewer hours, plus about 3 hours for adjudication at a 20% disagreement rate; to be re-costed with the counts); census threshold **15** (a stratum with at most 15 rows is reviewed whole); floor **10** rows per other stratum and **20** for any empty/null/unknown evidence-list stratum (always sampled, never assumed); the rest of the budget is shared in proportion to the square root of stratum size, capped at the stratum size. If census plus floors exceed the budget the plan stops and comes back to Kumar; it never shrinks silently.
   - **Draw:** row ids sorted ascending, then `numpy.random.default_rng([seed, crc32(stratum key)])` permutation, first n; the algorithm is pinned by a test.
   - **Reading the result:** because small strata are over-sampled, the headline is the **unweighted agreement on the reviewed sample**, labelled with its sample size; a second, **inverse-probability-weighted estimate for all 1,015 rows** (weight N_s / n_s per stratum) is reported beside it, labelled as an estimate. Neither is clinical accuracy.
   - **Approval gate:** the per-stratum table (stratum, N, n) computed from the real counts is brought to Kumar with this text before any row is drawn or read.
8. **Handling.** All review work stays local in gitignored storage; decisions are stored as row id plus decision only; no text in logs, reports, commits, PR text or model prompts; no model sees these notes in this protocol.
9. **Not part of this protocol:** running TrialLens, computing any agreement, or publishing anything. Those follow approval of the mapping and this protocol, with a separately approved paid ceiling for any model call.
